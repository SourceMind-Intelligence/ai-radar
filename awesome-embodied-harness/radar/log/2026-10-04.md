# Radar · 2026-10-04

## What happened

arXiv publishes no new listings over the weekend, and the scripted scan found 1 unlisted candidate above threshold (EgoTools, skipped earlier as out of scope). The run therefore promoted 12 of the 14 papers queued as "verified; over the daily cap" from 2026-09-29 to 2026-10-03, after re-opening each abstract and re-checking every code and project link. The queue is down to 2.

## Signals

- **Robustness evaluation is moving past success rate.**
  - Blackout vs. Freeze shows that two camera faults with similar success rates cause different physical harm.
  - The VLA behavioural-robustness study finds that equal success can hide very different trajectories.
  - RoboIRGBench shows VLAs collapse when the target is only referred to implicitly.
  - The Universal Adversarial Object (ICRA 2026) degrades Pi0 and RDT with one textured sphere in view.

  Together with LIBERO-MAX and the benchmark-bug audit (2026-10-01), the evaluation layer of the harness is getting its own adversarial test suite.
- **Memory is being split into explicit and implicit routes.** EvolvingNav keeps an explicit, probabilistic 4D belief over objects that move while unseen. T2Mem instead learns memory as fast weights inside the VLA. Both target RoboMME-style history dependence, from opposite ends of the harness/policy boundary.

## Added

- **COMPASS** (multi-agent): decentralized feedback control of up to 1,024 LLM-driven drones through learned spatial-transformer tokens. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28247)
- **EvolvingNav** (memory): a predictive 4D belief for navigation in worlds that change while unobserved; introduces EvoWorld-Bench. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39166)
- **T2Mem** (memory): test-time-trained fast-weight memory inside a VLA. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36720)
- **WorldAuditBench** (benchmark): multimodal agents auditing 3D worlds for anomalies. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40325)
- **RoboIRGBench** (benchmark): implicit referential grounding for VLAs. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34384)
- **OmniEcho** (benchmark): a spatial audio-visual benchmark and model for embodied agents. Data and evaluation code are still marked "coming soon". [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23407)
- **VLA behavioural robustness** (benchmark): behaviour metrics for successful trajectories under perturbation. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01351)
- **Universal Adversarial Object** (safety): a physical adversarial sphere against VLAs (ICRA 2026). [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39178)
- **Blackout vs. Freeze** (safety): physical failure modes of VLAs under camera faults. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39145)
- **Generative Physical AI review** (survey): a five-family taxonomy of generative physical AI. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18111)
- **ActionPiece** (policy): a relation-preserving action tokenizer for autoregressive VLAs; code released. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18487)
- **MATE** (data): distributed virtual teleoperation for multi-humanoid collaboration data. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26520)
