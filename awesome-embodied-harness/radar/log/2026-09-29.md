# Radar · 2026-09-29 (bootstrap)

## What happened

- **Initial import.** Nine research agents swept arXiv, lab blogs, GitHub and Hugging Face, one slice of the taxonomy each. Their results were merged and de-duplicated into **879 entries**.
  - Four rows were dropped:
    - `fast`, the same paper and system as `pi0-fast`;
    - `rosclaw-runtime`, the same project as `rosclaw-multi-agent`;
    - a duplicate REAL-I challenge row;
    - one low-signal awesome list.
  - Three pairs of different papers that shared an id were split: ROSClaw, RoboHarness, and the world-action-model surveys.
- **First scripted scan.** `awesome.py scan --since 2026-09-15` listed 45 candidates:
  - 11 added (below);
  - 12 queued in `radar/candidates.yaml` as over the daily cap;
  - the rest skipped.
- **Verification.** Every arXiv ID and title was checked against the arXiv API, and every link was checked over HTTP (see `check-links`). A few sites block bots (pi.website, openai.com, iso.org); those links were confirmed by the research agents through other channels.

## Signals

- **"Harness" is now an explicit research frame for embodied agents.** 29 embodied systems from 2026 carry the word in their title, 15 of them from September alone. Examples: Thea, Show-Harness, HarnessPAI, HarnessVLN, NavHarness, RegenHarness, AdaHVLA, MaskHarness-WAM. The pattern repeats the coding-agent story: the loop, tools, world state and exit codes around a fixed model are where systems now differ.
- **Verification is the weak link, and this week's answers are explicit world models.**
  - FailBench found VLM success judges reach at most 0.77 balanced accuracy.
  - ARS found reward baselines crediting progress for manipulating the wrong object.
  - GAVEL (a graph world model that checks LLM plans before execution) and HALTER (a scene graph for autonomous long-horizon resets) both make world state explicit to get reliable exit codes.
- **VLAs are being wrapped, not replaced.** SafeLoop adds hazard prediction and rollback, CARE adds corrective execution, and SEES adds failure-guided self-evolution, all around frozen or lightly adapted VLAs. Grounded Action Model is explicitly designed to also serve as the low-level controller under a VLM planner.

## Added

- **GAVEL** (verification): verifies and repairs LLM task plans against a graph world model before execution. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19315)
- **CARE** (verification): learns VLA recovery from the policy's own failed rollouts; adds FSR-Bench. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24118)
- **ARS** (verification): agentic, training-free progress reward modeling with general VLMs. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34484)
- **SafeLoop** (safety): hazard prediction and joint-space rollback around a VLA (IROS 2026). [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26313)
- **ShieldVLA** (safety): Hamilton-Jacobi reachability critic that gates VLA fine-tuning. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13231)
- **HALTER** (benchmark): harness for autonomous long-horizon evaluation and reset. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19413)
- **RoboFollow** (benchmark): shows high success rates can hide weak instruction following. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25636)
- **SEES** (harness): self-evolving system that targets the weakest atomic VLA skills with simulated RL. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32698)
- **X-Planner** (brain): X Square Robot's event-structured planning front-end for VLAs, with open 9B weights. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25187)
- **Grounded Action Model** (policy): 3D-grounded robot foundation model that can run under a VLM planner. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23863)
- **World Models for Embodied Intelligence** (survey): plausible → controllable → actionable. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16697)
