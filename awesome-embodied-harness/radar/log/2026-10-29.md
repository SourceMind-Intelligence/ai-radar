# Radar · 2026-10-29

## Signals

- **The harness wins again, this time on a low-cost arm.** SO-101 Bench builds an Isaac Lab digital twin of the SO-101 arm whose policy ranking matched the physical arm exactly. On it, an LLM agent that writes code and hands grasping to a learned policy scores 83.2%. The best of nine VLAs and world action models scores about 61%. An ablation attributes the lead to both the harness and the language model (first-party). RoboAuto searches robot programs with explicit object-, scene- and interaction-level physical knowledge, and runs self-designed experiments to settle physical uncertainty.
- **Safety wraps frozen policies from outside.**
  - VLA-Stem converts a frozen VLA's action chunks into motion references and adapts them around obstacles (52.8% versus 23.7% collision-free success, first-party).
  - A CoRL safety-workshop paper repairs corrupted sensor inputs around a frozen locomotion policy using a transparent surrogate.
  - OSMA-Bench finds that neither VLM-based nor VLA navigation policies consistently adjust clearance and speed to how hazardous an obstacle is.
- **Distilling agents into one compact embodied brain.** OmniBrain-0 distills strong agents' interaction experience into manipulation and navigation experts, then consolidates them into one decision model. A rubric-guided self-improvement flywheel keeps the cycle going. Code is announced.
