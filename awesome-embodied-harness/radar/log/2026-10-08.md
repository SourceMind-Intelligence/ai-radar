# Radar · 2026-10-08

## Signals

- **Harness optimization is now the explicit object of study.** EMHO keeps the embodied model frozen and rewrites the harness from execution traces: how the agent monitors progress, uses vision tools, grounds observations and responds to failure. It then merges per-subtask gains into one shared harness. VeriFine adds the missing piece, a judge that is improved together with the policy and curriculum, using humans only on informative failures. Together with last week's Scale and Selection and DynaHarness, the field is converging on "optimize the scaffold, gate the change, keep improving the verifier".
- **Guardrails for embodied agents fail in ordinary-looking cases.** What the Guard Misses shows that pi0.5 completes harmful-intent tasks whenever the intent is implied, that text guards miss up to 95% of such runs, and that harmful/harmless separability in activations weakens after robot training. The new jailbreak-guardrail benchmark is the first to compare guardrails at the perception, planning and control stages under identical attacks.
- **Coding agents are now baselines next to VLAs.** BiGym 2.0 benchmarks cold-start coding agents alongside VLA fine-tuning and RL on a humanoid. Agent-written programs beat every demo-driven RL baseline on the nine-task mean, though VLA fine-tuning still leads.

## Added

- **EMHO** (harness): self-optimizing harness for a frozen embodied model, built from experience traces, with EMHO-Merge for shared harnesses. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08432)
- **VeriFine** (verification): co-evolution of policy, curriculum and judge, with targeted human guidance. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08761)
- **Embodied jailbreak guardrails benchmark** (safety): six guardrails compared at the perception, planning and control stages. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06122)
- **Implied Harm in VLA Instructions** (safety): implied harmful intent passes both text guards and activation probes. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05818)
- **EmbodiedSmith** (sim): a recursive self-improvement flywheel for jointly generating assets, scenes and tasks. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07969)
- **SMART** (sim): articulation-aware synthetic data for zero-shot sim-to-real articulated manipulation. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07652)
- **OntoPlan** (memory): an ontology-grounded scene representation plus an agentic planner for large environments (NeurIPS 2026). [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07649)
- **BiGym 2.0** (benchmark): humanoid household manipulation comparing VLAs, imitation learning, RL and coding agents. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07594)
- **World Models' Last Exam in Physics** (benchmark): a 40-task, measurement-based physical-consistency test for video world models. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08791)
- **MRPilot** (multi-agent): mixed-reality supervision and intervention for LLM-driven robot teams. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07477)
- **RIWANav** (world-model): recursive co-improvement of a world model and a navigation policy. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08640)
- **RACE** (policy): transition-aware action-chunk extension for VLAs. Promoted from the queue. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05719)
