# Radar · 2026-10-13

## Signals

- **The self-evolution trend now has benchmarks.** EmbodiedCLBench is described as the first embodied continual-learning benchmark. It tests whether self-evolving agents can learn basic tasks and then recombine those skills on unseen compositional tasks, across several agent harnesses and learning methods. MAGMA-Bench stresses persistent multi-robot reasoning under injected failures, interruptions and mission updates through a skill-level interface. Together they give the past two weeks' self-evolving harnesses (EMHO, PhysEvo, EmbodiedRSI, DynaHarness) a common yardstick.
- **On-device agents keep safety checks out of the model.** BT-Agent runs a 9B model fully onboard a quadruped. Its behavior-tree skills enforce preconditions and can stop motion locally, without waiting for the next model decision. Robot-WATCHDOG (CoRL 2026) learns failure detection from nominal demonstrations alone, over object-centric scene graphs.
- **Motion quality near people is now measured on its own.** JitterVLA replays the same VLA command streams through different controllers to isolate jerk caused by delayed, irregular action arrival. On human hand trajectories with 200 ms delay, simple interpolation cut p99 jerk by 83% (first-party).

## Added

- **BT-Agent** (harness): an onboard LLM agent over behavior-tree skills with structured failure feedback, plus a multi-fidelity benchmark. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13862)
- **JoyInside-VLM-Agent** (harness): a product-conditioned ReAct multimodal agent trained with sandbox trajectories, distillation and RL. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12957)
- **EASG** (interface): explicit action-space grounding that turns a pretrained VLM into a continuous-action policy. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12832)
- **Robot-WATCHDOG** (verification): self-supervised failure detection over object-centric graphs (CoRL 2026). [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13403)
- **OGPO** (policy): goal-conditioned offline post-training for VLAs that recover from deviations (NeurIPS 2026). [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12914)
- **EmbodiedCLBench** (benchmark): continual learning and compositional skill reuse for self-evolving embodied agents. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13021)
- **MAGMA-Bench** (benchmark): long-horizon multi-robot task reasoning under dynamic tasks and failures. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13534)
- **AutoBio+** (benchmark): dexterous biology-lab manipulation with dynamic phenomena such as flames, vapor and bubbles. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13643)
- **JitterVLA** (benchmark): a replay benchmark for motion quality of VLA-driven robots near people; data released. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13628)
- **TouchScale** (data): 500 hours of unified-sensor human vision and touch; code and data released. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10288)
- **Splat2Act** (sim): phone-scan-to-Gaussian-splat digital twins that personalize a VLA to a user's space. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13945)
