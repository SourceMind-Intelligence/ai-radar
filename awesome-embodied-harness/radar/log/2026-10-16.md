# Radar · 2026-10-16

## Signals

- **The harness-versus-weights question gets an explicit answer.** AdaRSI starts with cheap harness and skill fixes. It retries persistent failures from saved states to find out whether the problem is how the VLA is called or what the VLA can do, and fine-tunes the executor only on the sub-steps that remain unreliable. This sits between the frozen-model harnesses (PhysEvo, EMHO) and policy co-evolution (Robo-COP) from the past two weeks. The new survey "Towards Agentic Robots" organizes the field along four axes, model, data, environment and harness. It complements the earlier "Towards Embodied Agentic AI" review already on the list.
- **Orchestration around a fixed VLA keeps getting more surgical.** ACRO uses a learned critic to decide when to stop a VLA attempt and where along the executed path to re-enter; trajectory retraction returns the robot there. Cracking Mobile Manipulation shows a subtask VLA dropping from 80% on atomic skills to 19% when composed into mobile manipulation, traces the loss to planning, navigation/base positioning and local manipulation, and adds targeted agentic interventions.
- **Evaluation is diversifying.** The newly promoted queue items include WorldOracle (world models checked against real executions), VLA-GAUGE (paired ID/OOD diagnosis), LIBERO-Industry (industrial process constraints), MultINav-Bench (navigation that requires opening doors and containers) and RoboFQE (rollout-free off-policy evaluation).

## Added

- **Towards Agentic Robots** (survey): a model, data, environment and harness survey of agentic robot systems. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20442)
- **AdaRSI** (harness): on-demand self-improvement that fine-tunes the executor only after failure attribution. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20474)
- **Cracking Mobile Manipulation** (harness): failure diagnosis plus agentic interventions around a fixed subtask VLA. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19600)
- **ACRO** (verification): actor–critic rollout orchestration with trajectory retraction for fixed VLAs. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19677)
- **RoboFQE** (verification): rollout-free policy evaluation from offline trajectories. Promoted from the queue. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18699)
- **ABot-ER** (brain): a unified embodied reasoning model with an agentic data-model self-evolution engine. Promoted from the queue. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18355)
- **CoSMO** (safety): open-world conformal semantic mapping for safe navigation (CoRL 2026). Promoted from the queue. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18321)
- **Observe, Share, or Predict?** (multi-agent): which partner information helps decentralized LLM planners. Promoted from the queue. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16895)
- **WorldOracle** (benchmark): action-conditioned world models compared against paired real executions. Promoted from the queue. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18826)
- **VLA-GAUGE** (benchmark): VLA generalization and robustness diagnosis. Promoted from the queue. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16567)
- **LIBERO-Industry** (benchmark): manipulation under industrial process constraints. Promoted from the queue. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16782)
- **MultINav-Bench** (benchmark): interactive navigation beyond reachability. Promoted from the queue. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17089)
