# Radar · 2026-10-09

## Signals

- **Self-evolving harnesses are learning which trials to run, and whether to touch the policy.**
  - Robo-COP argues that a harness around a frozen VLA can only route around the policy's failures. It fine-tunes the policy on curated deployment rollouts and re-tunes the orchestrator to match.
  - PhysEvo keeps GPT-6 Astra frozen. A meta-agent revises tools, skills and even its own diagnostic tools from trajectories.
  - EmbodiedRSI keeps competing code and skill hypotheses in a graph and picks physical experiments by value of information, so robot trials are spent where they discriminate most.
  - Agentic RSR applies the same loop to real-to-sim: an agent reconstructs the scene, then a coding agent develops and transfers the policy.
- **VLA security is getting runtime tooling.** TMT detects backdoor activation at runtime on unseen tasks. SOUL removes "state hallucination" behaviour through sparse-feature unlearning. RT-Safe makes decision latency part of the safety score. Rephrase Before You Act shows that a one-word rephrasing can swing pi0.5 from 100% to 2% on the same task, and fixes this by rewriting instructions with LLM-distilled rules, without retraining.
- **Milestone:** the list now holds 1,001 verified entries.

## Added

- **Robo-COP** (harness): co-evolution of the VLM orchestrator and the VLA policy during deployment. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09228)
- **PhysEvo** (harness): physical recursive self-improvement around a frozen GPT-6 Astra, driven by a self-improving meta-agent. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08995)
- **EmbodiedRSI** (harness): hypothesis-graph, value-of-information experiment selection for self-evolving code and skills. It is a different paper from EmbodiRSI. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10498)
- **Agentic RSR** (harness): agentic scene reconstruction plus coding-agent policy development for real-to-sim-to-real. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10479)
- **Evidence-driven HART for anomaly triage** (harness): human–agent–robot teaming over typed, bounded services for deep-space operations. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08933)
- **AeroEval** (verification): staged program and execution validation for LLM-generated drone missions. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09764)
- **TMT** (safety): runtime backdoor detection for VLAs on unseen tasks. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09462)
- **SOUL** (safety): sparse-feature unlearning of VLA state hallucination. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09496)
- **RoboQuest** (benchmark): goal-directed embodied exploration through search, inspection and testing. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10388)
- **RT-Safe** (benchmark): embodied-agent safety under real-time constraints. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09294)
- **OpenViTac** (benchmark): paired simulation and real visuo-tactile manipulation benchmark. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10384)
- **Rephrase Before You Act** (benchmark): VLA language sensitivity, mitigated by rule-based rephrasing. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10526)
