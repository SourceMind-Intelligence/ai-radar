# Radar · 2026-10-24

## Signals

- **Harnesses now decide when to supervise.** FastHarness has GPT-6 Astra review a frozen π0.5 policy. Stage memory and reviewed outcomes let it review less as experience accumulates, and reviewer tokens fall 57.2% on average (first-party). CARE has a lightweight critic escalate control from a fast local executor to a cloud model only when execution becomes unreliable. HiSTEVE brings the same pattern to Minecraft: a capability-aware harness with a completion detector tied to the policy. Supervision is becoming a budget to spend where it matters.
- **Monitoring moves inside the policy.** Progress Probes train linear probes on a VLA's own activations to flag executions that fall behind expected progress, with no external VLM. Echo (queued) and yesterday's post-hoc uncertainty heads point the same way.
- **Memory placement gets a pre-registered answer.** "Externalise the Memory" pre-registered its hypotheses before training. Distilling a memory-augmented teacher into LoRA policy weights gave no significant gain, while re-attaching the retrieval buffer at inference added 34 points on a reach task (first-party, one task).
- **Failure origins become measurable.** Alpha injects perception, actuation, kinematic and planner faults into LLM-driven manipulation, with a scripted oracle as a matched control. Dual Exam evaluates agents and world models separately before coupling them.
