## Signals

- **Harness evolution gets a curriculum.** The Trinity reorganizes tasks into a tree of shared capabilities. For each capability it builds simplified, verifiable simulated worlds (Capability-Driven Real2Sim2Real), evolves reusable harness modules along the tree, and routes new tasks to compatible harness configurations. Code is promised within a week of 28 Oct.
- **World models as counterfactual engines.** Rollback rewinds world-model rollouts to critical decision points and branches into alternative actions. Contrasting the outcomes yields action-level credit, which a physical environment cannot provide. Retrieve to Act takes the opposite route to robustness: it retrieves kinematic priors from the policy's own demonstrations rather than trusting shifted visual features.
