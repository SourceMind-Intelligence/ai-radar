# Radar · 2026-10-27

## Signals

- **Predicting failure from inside the policy, then acting on it.** Backtrack trains a small head (under 1% of parameters) on a frozen VLA's representations. When it predicts failure, the controller retraces recent motion and lets the unchanged policy retry, cutting LIBERO-Long failure rates by 19–48% across three VLAs (first-party). A CoRL safety-workshop paper shows sparse-autoencoder features of OpenVLA predict failure before it becomes unrecoverable. With last week's Progress Probes and post-hoc uncertainty heads, reading the policy's own activations is becoming a standard monitoring layer of the harness.
- **Memory for frontier planners becomes an attention budget.** LogLook keeps an execution log and earlier notes, but lets the planner decide which frames and demonstration segments to look at. It matches note-only memory with 43% fewer tokens and solves 93.5% of RoboMME with direct control (first-party).
- **Coding agents reach contact and real-to-sim.** FORCA writes force-responsive control programs from wrist force/torque readings and revises them from force records between real-robot runs. WorldMirror treats real-to-sim reconstruction as a world program that a coding agent refines from verification feedback.
- **Data engines and brains ship open.** NVIDIA's Flora, the labeling model suite behind GR00T data (rewards, progress and captions), releases weights under a research license. Alibaba DAMO's RynnUnify releases a 16B unified embodied model with agentic and world-action extensions.
